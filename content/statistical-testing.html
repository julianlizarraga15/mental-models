P-value

A p-value is a “surprise score” for your result if nothing real is happening.

Simple read:

Small p-value = “this result would be unusual as pure noise.” It does not prove the effect is big, important, or true.

Power analysis

Power analysis asks how many observations you need before the test has a good chance to notice a real effect.

One-screen memory hook

P-value
